Lesson 2

Empty is an answer

if not visible:

    raise ValueError("nothing up")

The run dies. No answer about the clouds, the Moon or the aurora either.

return {"visible": [],

        "note": "nothing up tonight"}

“No planets tonight, but it’s dark and clear. Look for the Milky Way instead.”

Throw, and the conversation stops. Return, and it carries on.

Short. Forty seconds. A night with no planets up isn't an error. It's a fact about tonight, and it's one the model can work with. It can still tell you the sky is dark and clear and point you at something else. Raise instead, and you don't just lose that tool. You lose the whole answer, including the three tools that worked. The rule: your tool should only raise when the caller genuinely can't continue. "I looked and there was nothing" isn't that.

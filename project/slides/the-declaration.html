This slide didn't exist until late and it's the one that makes lesson one inevitable. Spend a minute. Here is what you actually pass. Not a Python function. A dictionary. A name, a description, and the parameters. Read the description out loud, including the second half: zero is clear, above about forty there's no point going outside. That sentence isn't for you. It's the only thing telling the model what a useful answer looks like. Then the line on the right, and say it plainly: the model never sees your function body. It doesn't see your type hints, your tests, or what you meant. It sees three fields. Which is why the bottom line is true. A function called process, described as "processes data", isn't badly documented. It's invisible. The model has nothing to choose it by. Hold that thought. It comes back as lesson one with numbers attached.

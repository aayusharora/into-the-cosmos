Leaving it out is a decision too

A tool that comes back with nothing isn’t a wasted call. It’s why the answer is short.

Give this a whole slide and a whole beat. It's the best idea in the talk and it goes past people if you bury it in a list. The agent checks space weather. Kp is nought point six seven, which is about as quiet as it gets. There will be no aurora over north-east India tonight, and there was never going to be. So what does it do with that? Nothing. It doesn't mention aurorae at all. That's the bit worth sitting with. We spend all our time making agents produce more: more tools, more detail, more words. The good answer tonight is short precisely because one of the five calls told it what to leave out. If you want the general rule: a tool returning "no" hasn't failed and hasn't wasted your money. It has bought you a shorter, better answer. Most agent output is long because nobody taught it to be quiet.

Two bits of ceremony in the whole API. reply says which question you are answering. previous_interaction_id makes it one conversation.

Line two is the whole point of this talk. That is you calling your own function, with arguments Gemini chose. Nothing clever. You could put a print in there. Then you hand it back, and there are exactly two bits of ceremony in the entire API. reply is a four line helper that says WHICH question you are answering, by id, and what it returned. previous_interaction_id is what makes this the same conversation instead of two strangers talking. That is it. That is all the protocol there is. It is in the repo exactly like that.

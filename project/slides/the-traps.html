On your first attempt

Three things that’ll cost you an hour

call_id == step.id

Answer the exact step that asked. Get it wrong and the model never sees your result.

"result": [ … ]

A list of content blocks, not a string. This one surprises everybody.

response_format

Not response_schema. Every older example online says the wrong one.

None of these throw a useful error. They just quietly do nothing.

Reference slide. Forty seconds, don't explain them deeply. Promise the repo and move on. The call_id one: each thing the model did is a step with an id, and when you hand back a result you've to say which step you're answering. Get it wrong and your result goes nowhere; the model waits, then gives up. The result one catches everyone, including me. It's not a string. It's a list of content blocks, and if you pass a string it's simply ignored. And the third is a documentation problem rather than a design one. Structured output is response_format. A great deal of what is online says response_schema, because that's what the older API used, and it won't work. The bottom line is the reason this slide exists at all: none of these raise anything useful. They fail silently, which is the worst way for anything to fail, and you'll lose an evening to each one.

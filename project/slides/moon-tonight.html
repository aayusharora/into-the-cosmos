One sentence and let them read the chart. The shape says it without you. The white line is the Moon's height. It's below the horizon all evening and doesn't cross until three in the morning. Everything shaded is properly dark sky. So tonight is about as good as it gets: ten hours of darkness, and when the Moon finally turns up it's a thin crescent, fifteen per cent lit, which barely matters. The agent needs this because "is it clear" isn't the same question as "is it dark". You can have a perfectly clear sky and see almost nothing because there's a full Moon in it. That's thing five.

Why 4.31s? It calls Horizons once per planet. Four round trips, one after another.

Fire them off together, or work the whole night out before anybody asks.

Seven seconds of tools, several rounds deep, is a person watching a blank screen.

These are measured, on my laptop, on a good connection. Say that. The interesting one is planets_tonight. It looks like one tool and it's really four HTTP calls in a row, because I wrote a loop over the planets. Nobody reviews that and thinks "this is the slow one". You find out by timing it. Now put it in context. The model may call tools across several rounds. Seven seconds of tool time, two or three rounds deep, is twenty seconds of someone staring at nothing. Two fixes and they're both boring. Fan the four requests out concurrently. Or notice that the sky tonight is the same for everyone in this city, compute it once at sunset, and serve it from a row. The second one is the one that leads into the cloud section.

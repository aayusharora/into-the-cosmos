What’s up there tonight?

"https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/api/horizons.api"

  ?COMMAND='699'              # Saturn

  &SITE_COORD='88.40,26.73,0'  # Siliguri

  &QUANTITIES='4,9'           # how high, how bright

JPL will work out the sky from anywhere on Earth. No key needed.

Thirty seconds. Point at the three parameters, not the URL. Which body. Where you're standing. What you want to know: how high it is and how bright. That's a tool. Hand it to Gemini and it can look up at the sky from anywhere on the planet, on any night, past or future. Then turn the page.
